READING THE EXPERIMENT

Disagreement is a result,
not a diagnosis.

Single phase advances at a constant rate through each whole cycle. Dual phase allocates half the state range to expiration and half to inspiration. Amplitude state progresses with displacement and direction, using the known extrema of this invented cycle.

The cycle begins at maximum displacement, at the start of expiration. Its minimum marks the inspiration transition. State 1 wraps to state 0; circular distances account for that wrap. A pause can give one state a large occupancy without indicating a software error.

Each coloured strip beneath the signal identifies its empirical bin at each sample. The two tracks can differ even when continuous disagreement is below one bin width. Descriptive RMSD classification and exact bin agreement therefore answer different questions.

These are explicit educational definitions. They do not reproduce a scanner's proprietary sorting, infer internal anatomy, reconstruct images or calculate safe acquisition settings.
