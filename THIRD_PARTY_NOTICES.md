# Third-party references and optional dependencies

The VXP reader uses facts from the publicly accessible manufacturer format
description attributed in [the VXP documentation](docs/vxp-processing.md).
The manual itself is not included and is not covered by this project's license.

General time/phase and amplitude sorting concepts are referenced in
[the research definitions](docs/RESEARCH_METHODS.md). No paper text, example
recording, image or implementation is included. Referencing a paper does not
establish permission to redistribute a private application or research framework.

Python's standard library is used at runtime but is not bundled. Optional figure
generation uses Matplotlib and its installed dependencies, each under their own
licenses. They are not vendored in this repository. The included figure is newly
generated from the repository's entirely invented mathematical examples.

The repository's MIT license applies to its independently written source and
documentation. Vendor and project names identify references only and do not
imply endorsement or clinical validation.
